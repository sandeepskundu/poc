For a tech-based company running a super app (like Grab, WeChat, Paytm, Gojek), the departments are usually a mix of core tech, business ops, product verticals, 
and support functions — because such companies handle multiple services under one umbrella (payments, ride-hailing, e-commerce, food delivery, etc.).

Here’s a comprehensive department breakdown:

1. Executive & Strategy - EXE
    - Executive Leadership (CEO, COO, CTO, CPO, CFO, CMO, CIO, CHRO)
    - Corporate Strategy & Planning
    - Business Development / Partnerships
    - Investor Relations
    - Legal & Compliance
    - Corporate Communications / PR

2. Technology & Engineering - ENG
    - Backend Engineering (Microservices, APIs)
    - Frontend Engineering (Web, Mobile)
    - Mobile App Development (iOS, Android, Cross-platform)
    - DevOps / SRE (Site Reliability Engineering)
    - Cloud Infrastructure & Platform Engineering
    - Database Administration
    - Data Engineering
    - AI / Machine Learning / NLP
    - Cybersecurity / InfoSec
    - Quality Assurance / Test Automation
    - R&D (Emerging Tech)

3. Product & Design - PRD
    - Product Management
    - UX/UI Design
    - User Research
    - Content Design / UX Writing
    - Growth & Experimentation Team
    - Localization / Internationalization

4. Data & Analytics - DAT
    - Data Science
    - Business Intelligence (BI)
    - Data Analytics
    - Data Governance
    - A/B Testing & Experimentation

5. Business Verticals (Service Lines) - (Each could have its own mini product/tech/ops team)
    - Payments & Fintech
    - E-commerce / Marketplace
    - Food Delivery
    - Ride-hailing / Mobility
    - Logistics / Courier
    - Travel & Bookings
    - Entertainment / Streaming
    - Healthcare / Telemedicine
    - Education / e-Learning
    - Social / Messaging
    - Advertising & Marketing Solutions

6. Operations & Customer Support - OPS | CXS
    - Customer Experience / Support (CX)
    - Operations Management
    - Vendor Management
    - Merchant Onboarding
    - Driver / Partner Operations
    - Fraud Detection & Risk Ops

7. Sales & Marketing - SAL | MKT
    - Performance Marketing
    - Brand Marketing
    - Content Marketing
    - Social Media
    - Field Sales
    - Partner Sales
    - AdTech & Media Buying

8. Finance & Admin - HRT 
    - Accounting
    - Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A)
    - Procurement
    - Billing & Collections
    - Investor Relations
    - Tax & Compliance

9. Human Resources & Culture - CXS
    - Talent Acquisition
    - Learning & Development
    - Employee Engagement
    - Compensation & Benefits
    - Workplace Experience / Facilities
    - HR Operations

10. Special Initiatives
    - Innovation Lab
    - Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR)
    - Sustainability & ESG
    - Internal Audit
    - Government Relations


| Department (Main)             | Code  | Description                                 |
| ----------------------------- | ----- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Executive Leadership          | `EXE` | CEO, COO, strategic leadership              |
| Product & Design              | `PRD` | Product management, UX/UI design            |
| Engineering & Development     | `ENG` | Backend, frontend, mobile, QA, DevOps       |
| Data & AI                     | `DAT` | Data science, analytics, AI/ML              |
| Sales & Partnerships          | `SAL` | Sales, partnerships, business development   |
| Marketing & Communications    | `MKT` | Digital marketing, PR, branding             |
| Customer Experience & Support | `CXS` | Customer success, support, retention        |
| HR & Talent Management        | `HRT` | Recruitment, onboarding, employee relations |
| Finance & Legal               | `FIN` | Finance, accounting, legal, compliance      |
| Operations & Admin            | `OPS` | Office admin, logistics, procurement        |
| Security & Compliance         | `SEC` | Cybersecurity, data protection, compliance  |
| R\&D & Innovation             | `RDI` | Research, emerging tech, prototyping        |
