🧍‍♂️ Personal Information
    - Full Name (first name, last name)
    - Gender (optional)
    - Date of Birth
    - Nationality
    - Profile Photo (optional but useful for personalization)
    - Preferred Language 

📞 Contact Information
    - Email Address (primary identifier)
    - Mobile Number (with country code)
    - Alternate Phone Number (optional)
    - Residential Address (for billing or verification)

🔐 Account Details
    - Username
    - Password (hashed & secured)
    - Account Type (e.g., guest, registered, corporate, agent)
    - Login Method (email/password, Google, Facebook, Apple, etc.)
    - KYC/ID Proof Upload (if required for certain bookings)

✈️ Travel Preferences
    - Preferred Airport/City
    - Seat Preference (aisle/window)
    - Meal Preferences (veg/non-veg, allergies)
    - Loyalty Program Numbers (e.g., airline or hotel reward memberships)
    - Frequent Travel Destinations
    - Preferred Currency

📜 Booking & History Data
    - Past Bookings (with booking references)
    - Saved Itineraries or Wishlists
    - Saved Payment Methods (tokenized for security)
    - Travel Insurance Preference
    - Special Requests (e.g., wheelchair, early check-in)

📧 Communication Preferences
    - Marketing Preferences (opt-in/out for promotions)
    - Preferred Communication Channel (email, SMS, WhatsApp)

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family & Travel Companions
    - Saved Traveler Profiles (for frequent co-travelers like spouse, children)

🔍 Security & Activity

    - Session Logs
    - Two-Factor Authentication Status
    - Consent Logs (for GDPR or data usage)
